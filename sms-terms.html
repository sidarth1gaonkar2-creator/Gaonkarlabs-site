SMS Program Terms

Gaonkar Labs LLC · Effective August 15, 2026

Program Description

Gaonkar Labs LLC sends appointment confirmation and reminder text messages for customers who book appointments through our AI booking assistant. Messages are transactional only — we do not send marketing or promotional texts.

Opt-In

You opt in by verbally agreeing during a phone call when our assistant asks: "Would you like to receive text messages from Gaonkar Labs confirming your appointment? You'd receive up to two messages per appointment — one confirmation and one reminder. Message and data rates may apply, and you can opt out anytime by replying STOP." Messages are sent only if you clearly say yes. Your consent is recorded in the call transcript. We send messages only to the number you provide.

Message Frequency

Typically one to two messages per appointment (a confirmation, and where applicable a reminder). Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. You will receive a final message confirming the opt-out, and no further messages will be sent. Reply HELP for help, or contact sidarthgaonkar@gaonkarlabs.com.

Consent is not a condition of booking any appointment or purchasing any service.

Privacy

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the text message services. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
