What it does

Every call answered. Every booking captured. Anything urgent, escalated.

Answers as your practice

Your services, your hours, your policies — from a knowledge base built for your practice, not generic scripts. Callers hear your front desk, not a bot reading a menu.

Books real appointments

Checks live availability, confirms date and time back, reads phone numbers back digit by digit, and sends a confirmation text the moment the booking lands.

Knows when to hand off

A caller describing a post-treatment concern gets a provider callback arranged immediately — never medical advice from a machine. Requests it can’t handle go to your team with a name and number.

What it deliberately won’t do: quote prices, give clinical advice, collect medical history, or guess. Every call is transcribed and reviewable, and consent is asked before any text is sent. Inbound calls only — no outbound anything.
